## CS 250 - Reflection

### How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating “user stories” help with this?
I interpret user needs by breaking them down into clear user stories. User stories help me understand who the user is, what they want, and why they want it. This makes it easier to design features that actually solve their problem.

### How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work?
I approach development in small, iterative steps. Agile processes like sprints, reviews, and retrospectives help me stay organized and adjust quickly when requirments change. I plan to keep using these practices because they make development more flexable and predictable.

### What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?
A good team member communicates clearly, shares progress, and supports the rest of the team. It means being reliable, open to feedback, and willing to collaborate so the whole team can deliver quality work.
